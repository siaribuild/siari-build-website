About Us

BUILT ON DETAIL.

Fourteen years of doing it properly — architectural precision, real craftsmanship, and a builder who still walks every site.

OUR STORY

Siari Build started where a lot of good builders start — on the tools. Small renovations in Melbourne's west, then our first two-unit sites, built for ourselves before we ever built them for clients.

As the work got bigger, the standard didn't change. Today we deliver custom homes, bespoke designs, multi-dwelling developments and high-end renovations across Victoria — but every project still gets the same hands-on attention those first jobs did.

We only work with Grade-A tradespeople we trust, we keep you in the loop at every stage, and we're honest when plans change — because on a real build, they sometimes do. That openness is the whole reason clients refer us to the people they care about.

The name? It's our two boys, Sian and Ari. Building things that outlast us is personal here — it's literally over the door.
