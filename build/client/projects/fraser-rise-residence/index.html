Overview

The Fraser Rise Residence is a contemporary double-storey custom home designed to combine modern architecture with practical family living. At approximately 300sqm, this residence showcases clean architectural lines, quality finishes and thoughtfully designed living spaces, delivering a home that is both functional and timeless.

Constructed through Siari Build’s complete design-to-handover service, the project reflects our commitment to quality craftsmanship, attention to detail and delivering exceptional outcomes for our clients.

The Challenge

The goal was to create a modern family home that balanced contemporary design with everyday practicality while remaining within the client’s budget. Achieving premium finishes, functional living spaces and efficient construction required careful planning, strong project management and close coordination with consultants and trades throughout every stage of the build.

Our Solution

Siari Build managed every stage of the project, from planning and permits through to construction and final handover. By maintaining strict quality control and open communication throughout the build, we delivered a beautifully finished custom home that exceeded the client’s expectations while maintaining a smooth and efficient construction process.
