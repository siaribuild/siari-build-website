Overview

The Tiffany Walk Residence is a luxury custom home designed to deliver timeless architecture, exceptional craftsmanship and resort-style family living. Spanning approximately 560sqm (60 Squares), this residence showcases premium finishes, expansive living spaces and carefully considered architectural details throughout.

Positioned within the prestigious Sanctuary Lakes estate overlooking the golf course, the home seamlessly integrates indoor and outdoor living through floor-to-ceiling glazing, a resort-style swimming pool and spa, and generous entertaining spaces. Every aspect of the residence has been meticulously designed to create a home that is both luxurious and functional.

The Challenge

The vision was to create a statement residence that maximised its premium golf course location while delivering uncompromising luxury and everyday practicality. The design required expansive open-plan living, abundant natural light, seamless indoor-outdoor integration and premium architectural detailing throughout, while maintaining privacy, energy efficiency and timeless appeal.

Executing these design ambitions demanded meticulous planning, precision construction and exceptional attention to detail across every stage of the project.

Our Solution

Working closely with the client from concept through to completion, Siari Build delivered a bespoke residence that combines sophisticated architectural design with superior craftsmanship.

The home features a dramatic double-height entrance, a custom spiral staircase, premium natural stone finishes, bespoke joinery and expansive floor-to-ceiling glazing that frames the surrounding golf course. A resort-style swimming pool and spa create a seamless connection between the indoor living spaces and the outdoor entertaining area, while every material and finish was carefully selected to deliver a timeless, luxurious home.

By managing every stage of the project—from design coordination and approvals through to construction and final handover—we delivered a residence that exceeds expectations in both quality and design.
